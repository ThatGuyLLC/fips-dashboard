2026-11-03 FIL Implementer Call
Nov 3, 2026

Nv30 Scoping

NV29NV28FIP-0107FIP-0117FIP-0118FIP-0119

Upgrades and FIPs mentioned. Mentions are not decisions.

Meeting notes (5 topics, 11 points)

NV30 Scope: Primary Driver

  • FIP-0119: increase max sector lifetime from 5 to 10 years
    • Main urgency driver for NV30 timing
    • ~118 PiB hard-capped in the affected window; ~76 PiB expiring through Nov 2026, ~87 PiB through March 2027
    • ~42 PiB unextendable without this FIP; ~268 PiB/year resealing required to hold capacity flat
    • Activating in early December preserves ~42 PiB; January ~40 PiB; March ~31 PiB

NV30 Scope: Small Additions

  • Fault fees and reward-derived charges after Solstice (discussion #1302)
    • Default fees tied to full block reward; as rewards decline, fault fees could become ~2x appropriate amount
    • dProposal: carry consensus weight into miner-actor charges using max(w1, 0.5); ~214,000 FIL in fault fees over last year
    • Small parameter change; beneficial for SPs (lower fault fees over time)
    • Wouldn't drive an upgrade, but easy to include if NV30 is happening
  • Removing legacy Fil+ actors (f06, f07, f080)
    • Frozen at NV29 by FIP-0118; state occupies ~11.8 GB across 14M blocks
    • Rod opened a PR; low-lift cleanup candidate
  • FilOz to do a fresh pass on state cleanup and migration speed improvements
    • Light items could opportunistically join NV30; nothing held up for this

NV30 Scope: Deferred or Out

  • FIP-0117 (repeated SnapDeals with piece-level integrity): L-sized, requires heavy SP testing; too large for this upgrade
  • Fork Signaling Repurposing: deferred since NV28; non-consensus-critical, likely an FRC, not worth the lift given other priorities
  • Anything above medium t-shirt size: not targeting for NV30
  • FIP-0107 (implicit messages in block receipts), Sealer ID, F3 Calibration Smart Contract Activation: deferred to NV30 but not discussed as active candidates

Ownership and Decision-Making

  • Business decision on upgrade timing sits outside this meeting; engineering can be reactive given small implementation lift
  • Suggested outreach: identify miner IDs dropping power in Nov/Dec, contact them to gauge willingness to extend sectors if FIP-0119 passes

Next Steps

  • Move FIP-0119 to Last Call No objections raised; opening the PR after this meeting.

About the implementers' calls

Filecoin implementation teams meet regularly to coordinate network upgrades: scope, timelines, test vectors, calibration and mainnet readiness, and blockers on accepted FIPs. Core Devs sets priorities; these calls are where implementers work out how and when they ship.

NV29 Retrospective and Template Improvements

  • Lessons-learned doc open for contributions while context is fresh
  • Key items already captured (from screenshots)
    • Use Google Doc variables for upgrade issue links
    • Record Butterfly host shutdowns and state changes post-testing
    • Document reset prerequisites
    • Capture calibration snapshots within a few hours of upgrade
  • Doc format discussion: Google Doc tables are hard for agent interaction; Notion considered but permissions management is tricky
    • Filoz doing more doc work via agents, so agent-navigability is a real factor

FIP-0119: Increase Maximum Sector Lifetime to 5 → 10 Years

  • Draft FIP (PR #1293, lucaniz) proposes raising max sector lifetime for PoRep v1.1, SyntheticPoRep, and NI-PoRep
  • No state migration, no pledge/fee/proof changes required
  • Motivation: ~42 PiB cannot be extended after December 2026; 58 PiB expiring Nov 1, 76 PiB by month-end
  • Security risks noted: PoRep flaws and cost/latency drift narrowing SDR margins
  • Caveat: market actors still cap at 3.5 years, so archival terms offered to clients may not be binding

Network Upgrade Cadence

  • Historical norm: ~3 upgrades/year; concern raised about exchange exhaustion
  • Coinbase already cautious; every upgrade creates operational burden for exchanges
  • Consensus: no more than 2 major upgrades per year, with a predictable published schedule
    • Analogy: Ubuntu’s fixed release dates give downstream maintainers a reliable target
    • Cardano cited: 1 major upgrade/year due to long dev cycles and on-chain governance
  • Ad hoc upgrades remain possible for emergencies
  • Proposal: bring to Core Devs call to establish 2 official dates per year; FIPs available at that point determine whether the upgrade proceeds

Wasmtime Dependency Update

  • Good time to do dependency updates now that NV29 is out, to let things bake before NV30
  • Wasmtime v47 upgrade brings ~10% speedup for any state computation
  • Filoz planning to spend time on this; Wasmtime update in ref-FVM had been blocked on Rob, who is now re-engaging

Next Steps

  • Check for existing FIL+ deprecation blog post and share link
  • Check in with Filoz team on Solstice SP messaging
    • Confirm what blog content is coming out and surface feedback from today's sync.
  • Incorporate NV29 lessons learned into upgrade template
    • Invite others to add observations to the doc while context is fresh.
  • Raise network upgrade cadence on Core Devs call
    • Propose 2 official upgrade dates per year with ad hoc exceptions for emergencies.
  • Progress Wasmtime v47 upgrade in ref-FVM
